# Skill: Calwood HD Competitive Intelligence

AI skill for monitoring and analyzing competitor activity in the Home Depot flooring category.

## What belongs here

- Skill definition files (skill configuration, prompts, tool definitions)
- Competitor price tracking logic and comparison templates
- Assortment gap analysis scripts and prompts
- SKU-level positioning data and scoring rubrics
- Alerts and triggers for significant competitor price or availability changes

## Intended use

This skill ingests competitor product and pricing data (from HomeDepot.com search results, third-party scrapers, or manual uploads) and surfaces actionable intelligence: where Calwood is priced out of range, where assortment gaps exist, and where competitors are gaining shelf placement.

# Docs: Weekly Review

Templates and archives for Calwood's weekly Home Depot channel review cadence.

## What belongs here

- Weekly review meeting templates (agenda, KPI dashboard, action items)
- Completed weekly review archives (organized by date)
- Channel health scorecards (sales velocity, fill rate, dispute backlog, open quotes)
- Action item trackers carried forward from prior weeks
- Notes from buyer calls, store visits, or partner meetings

## Naming convention

`YYYY-MM-DD-weekly-review.md`
Example: `2025-06-02-weekly-review.md`

Weekly reviews should cover: sales vs. target, open disputes and freight claims, Rithum fill rate and exceptions, QuoteCenter pipeline, Pro Desk outreach activity, and SKU/catalog updates.

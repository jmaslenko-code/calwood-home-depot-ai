# Templates: Store Outreach

Communication templates for engaging Home Depot store associates, Pro Desk staff, department managers, and district leadership.

## What belongs here

- Pro Desk introduction and relationship-building email templates
- In-store visit follow-up templates
- Product knowledge one-pagers and leave-behind formats
- Special Order program introduction scripts
- District manager and DM-level outreach templates
- Event and training invitation templates

## Naming convention

`[audience]-[purpose]-[version].md`
Example: `pro-desk-intro-v1.md`, `dm-quarterly-update-v2.md`

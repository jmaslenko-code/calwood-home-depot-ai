# Templates: Rithum / CommerceHub Analysis

Templates for analyzing and reporting on Rithum (formerly CommerceHub) order and fulfillment data.

## What belongs here

- Order volume and fill rate analysis templates
- Fulfillment exception and error review templates
- EDI transaction reconciliation checklists
- Carrier performance summary templates
- Rithum scorecard and compliance metric templates
- Month-over-month trend report templates

## Intended use

These templates standardize how Rithum data exports are structured and interpreted. Use them as starting points when preparing fulfillment reviews, identifying integration issues, or reporting channel health to internal stakeholders.

# Templates: QuoteCenter

Templates for managing Home Depot QuoteCenter bids, special pricing requests, and project quote workflows.

## What belongs here

- Quote submission templates and bid cover sheets
- Special pricing justification templates (project size, competitive match, contractor loyalty)
- QuoteCenter response and follow-up email templates
- Win/loss debrief templates for completed bids
- Contractor and Pro customer quote history summaries

## Intended use

QuoteCenter is Home Depot's system for contractor and project-based pricing. These templates support the end-to-end quoting process: preparing competitive bids, communicating with store staff and contractors, and capturing outcome data to improve future win rates.

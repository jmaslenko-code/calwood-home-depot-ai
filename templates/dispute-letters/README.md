# Templates: Dispute Letters

Reusable letter and email templates for Home Depot invoice disputes, chargebacks, and compliance deductions.

## What belongs here

- Chargeback dispute letter templates (by deduction type: shortage, compliance, freight, returns)
- Escalation letter templates for unresolved or aged disputes
- Supporting documentation cover sheets
- Response templates for Home Depot vendor compliance notifications
- Win/loss tracking notes tied to specific template versions

## Naming convention

`[deduction-type]-[audience]-[version].md`
Example: `shortage-dispute-buyer-v1.md`, `freight-correction-ap-v2.md`

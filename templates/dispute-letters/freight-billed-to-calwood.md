# Dispute Template: Freight Billed to Calwood — Home Depot Should Pay

## When to use this template

Use this template when Calwood has been billed for freight — by FedEx, an LTL carrier, or through a Home Depot remittance deduction — on a shipment where Home Depot was responsible for the freight cost under:

- The PO freight terms (collect, third-party bill-to)
- Home Depot's vendor routing guide
- A pre-negotiated freight arrangement where HD arranges and pays the carrier

Common scenarios where this template applies:

- Home Depot issued a **collect** PO but the carrier billed Calwood's account instead
- Home Depot's routing guide required HD to arrange the carrier, but the carrier billed Calwood
- A **FedEx third-party billing** charge was routed to Calwood's account without authorization
- An **LTL carrier** listed Calwood as the bill-to party when the PO terms say collect or third-party
- Home Depot deducted freight from Calwood's remittance on a shipment where HD was supposed to pay
- Accessorial charges (liftgate, residential delivery, redelivery) were billed to Calwood when the delivery was to a Home Depot distribution center or store (commercial address, dock available)

## When NOT to use this template

Do not use this template if:

- The PO freight terms are prepaid — Calwood is responsible for freight on prepaid terms
- Calwood selected a carrier outside the routing guide and is being charged for the routing violation
- The freight charge is a legitimate Calwood cost that was simply invoiced separately
- The billing error is Calwood's (e.g., Calwood provided the wrong bill-to account to the carrier)

## Evidence Checklist

Gather these before submitting.

- [ ] Carrier invoice showing the charge and the billed account
- [ ] Original PO showing freight terms (collect, third-party, or prepaid)
- [ ] Home Depot routing guide section confirming HD pays freight for this lane or shipment type (if applicable)
- [ ] Tracking number or PRO number linking the charge to the specific shipment
- [ ] Proof of delivery confirming the shipment reached the correct HD store or DC
- [ ] Any carrier rate confirmation or freight quote showing agreed billing terms
- [ ] Prior remittance showing the deduction (if HD deducted it rather than carrier billing directly)

**Strong case:** PO says collect or third-party, carrier billed Calwood's account instead. Submit immediately.
**Medium case:** Freight terms are ambiguous or routing guide reference is unclear. Confirm terms before submitting.
**Do not submit:** If PO terms are prepaid and Calwood is responsible for the charge.

## Notes

- **FedEx third-party billing errors** are common when shipping software defaults to the shipper's account instead of the third-party bill-to account on the PO. Audit every FedEx invoice against the PO freight terms.
- **LTL reclassification charges** are a separate issue — if the carrier upgraded the freight class and billed Calwood the difference, that is a separate dispute about the freight class, not about who pays. Use the freight class dispute approach separately.
- **Accessorial charges** (liftgate, residential, redelivery) billed on deliveries to Home Depot stores or DCs are almost always invalid — HD stores have loading docks and are commercial addresses. Flag these for dispute using this template.
- Always pull the routing guide section that covers the specific origin-to-destination lane. Routing guide requirements can differ by region, shipment size, and product type.
- If you cannot locate the routing guide, request a copy from your Home Depot buyer or vendor relations contact before submitting — you want to cite the specific section.

# SKU Gap Analysis Template

Use this template when a competitive scan identifies a product category where competitors have Home Depot listings and Calwood does not — or where Calwood's coverage is significantly weaker than the competition.

One template per gap. Be specific. A "white oak stair tread" gap is a different analysis from a "white oak stair nose" gap. Do not combine unrelated gaps into one template.

Label all data: **Confirmed**, **Estimated**, or **Unknown**.


### Revenue confidence check

Do not present a revenue estimate as reliable if:

- Fewer than 2 comparable Calwood SKUs exist to benchmark against
- Competitor review count is under 10 (insufficient demand signal)
- Calwood's cost structure has not been confirmed
- The demand signal is based on competitor presence alone with no Pro Desk or search volume confirmation

Process

How to Order Through Home Depot

All Calwood products are available via Special Order, QuoteCenter, and Pro Desk.

1

Choose Flooring or Sample

Customer selects a Calwood flooring product or requests a take-home sample from HomeDepot.com or in-store.

2

Collect Project Details

Home Depot associate collects project details: rooms, square footage, stair count, accessory needs, and timeline.

3

Contact Calwood or Use Special Order / QuoteCenter

Associate contacts Calwood directly, or submits through Home Depot Special Order or QuoteCenter portal.

4

Calwood Confirms & Quotes

Calwood confirms product availability, matching accessories, lead time, and provides a complete project quote.

5

Customer Orders Through Home Depot

Order is placed through Home Depot’s normal Special Order or QuoteCenter process.
